Privacy, in plain words

Last updated on 20 November 2026. This policy covers the Lapmend app for iPhone.

The short version

Your workouts stay on your iPhone. Lapmend has no server, no account and no ads, and its developer never sees your health data.

Health data

With your permission, Lapmend reads from Apple Health your workouts and, for each one, its route, distance, active and resting energy and heart rate. It uses them for one thing: to show you the workout and build the corrected version you ask for.

When you save, Lapmend writes the corrected workout to Apple Health as a new workout with the same kinds of data. It cannot change or delete data recorded by your Apple Watch or by other apps, and it does not try to.

  • Health data is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and is never sold or shared with data brokers.
  • Health data is not stored in iCloud by Lapmend. The app keeps on your iPhone only a short list of the workouts it corrected, so it can remind you to delete the originals.

You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app, under your profile, Apps and Services, Lapmend.

GPX files

Exporting the route of a corrected workout as a GPX file is free. The file contains the places, times and heart rate of that workout, and it goes only where you send it from the iOS share sheet.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To keep a record of them, Lapmend uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase history of the app. It receives no health data, no location and nothing that says who you are. See RevenueCat's policy.

Anonymous usage counters

The app counts, anonymously, how many times the Pro page and the Pro suggestion after a corrected workout were shown and closed, where the Pro page was opened from (for example the third piece of a join or the treadmill correction) and how many times the app asked for a rating. These counters are sent to RevenueCat with the same anonymous identifier, so that the developer can understand how the app is used and improve it. They never include your workouts, their routes, dates, distances or heart rate, any health data, your location or anything you type. They are not linked to you and they are not used for tracking.

What is not in the app

No tracking and no third-party analytics tools: the only counters are the anonymous ones described above. No advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Lapmend is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Workouts it wrote stay in Apple Health, where you can delete them from the Fitness or Health app, or from inside Lapmend before uninstalling.
