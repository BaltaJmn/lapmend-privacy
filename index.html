Forgot to stop? Cut the tail

Lapmend is an iPhone app that fixes Apple Health workouts: it trims the minutes after you really stopped and joins workouts that got split in two or three.

Get Lapmend on the App Store

How it works

Open a workout and Lapmend draws its pace minute by minute, marks where you stopped, and shows the route and the numbers before and after. You decide where the cut goes.

Apple Health does not let an app change or delete a workout recorded by your Watch. Lapmend saves the corrected workout as a new one and guides you through deleting the old one in the Fitness app, then checks that it is gone so nothing is counted twice.

Questions

Does it need an account? No. There is no sign-up and no server.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. See the privacy policy.

What does Pro add? Joining three or more workouts of any length and treadmill distance correction. Exporting the route of a corrected workout as a GPX file is free.
